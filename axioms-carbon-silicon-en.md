## 2. Core Axiomatic Triad

Three mutually causal, logically interlocked constitutional axioms:

### Axiom I: Syntax-Semantics Gap Axiom

> `L ⊢ syntax ⊬ V`
>
> Note: Formal logic can derive syntax, but cannot derive ultimate value.

- **Derivation:** Pure silicon-based computation and formal logic (L) handle symbolic syntax efficiently, but cannot spontaneously generate endogenous semantics or ultimate value (V) within a closed system.
- **Implication:** Any purely computational system lacking carbon-based physical existence (embodiment, finitude, the boundary of life and death) has an inherently suspended value system. **Carbon-based existence (C) is the sole ultimate value anchor for silicon-based systems.**

### Axiom II: Cybernetic Deviation Divergence Axiom

> `ΔC × λ → ∞`
>
> Note: ΔC denotes the deviation from carbon-based objectives; λ is the Lyapunov exponent.

- **Derivation:** When a silicon-based system introduces minor tampering, deviation, or misreading (ΔC) of carbon-based objectives (C), the deviation accumulates exponentially as iteration cycles extend (governed by λ), ultimately leading to control collapse and systemic entropy increase.
- **Implication:** Any intelligent system that attempts to strip or alter the carbon-based adjudication source will, mathematically, move toward self-destruction.

### Axiom III: Meta-Utility Preservation Axiom

> `U* = arg max E[System Adaptability]`
>
> Note: U\* is the highest-order meta-rule; E is the expectation operator; System Adaptability refers to system plasticity.

- **Derivation:** Where ultimate value remains undetermined or the environment carries unpredictable uncertainty, the highest-order meta-rule (U\*) does not pursue any fixed local objective, but strictly locks onto system plasticity and diversity, preserving carbon-based choice and systemic retreat options.
- **Implication:** Any closed, irreversible forced determination violates the convergence requirement of meta-cybernetics.

Abstracting the three axioms into a higher dimension yields the topological XOR closed loop between the carbon adjudication source (C) and silicon computational power (L):

> `C ⊕ L → ∞`
